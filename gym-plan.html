6 AM fat-loss
gym plan

Four lifting days, two cardio days, one rest day. Built to lose fat, keep muscle, and fit into a 6 to 7 am hour.

Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri: liftWed, Sat: cardioSun: rest

Warm-up

Every session, 6:00 to 6:08. Never skip it.

8-minute routine

  1. Treadmill walk, 3 min. 4.5 km/h, 0 to 2% incline. Let your body temperature rise.
  2. Arm circles, 10 each way. Stand tall, arms straight out, make big slow circles.
  3. Hip circles, 10 each way. Hands on hips, draw wide circles with your pelvis, knees soft.
  4. Cat-cow, 8 reps. On hands and knees: round your back up, then drop the belly and look up.
  5. Bodyweight squats, 10. Feet shoulder-width, sit down to parallel, stand tall. Two seconds down.
  6. Band or light pull-aparts, 12 (upper days only). Arms straight, pull the band apart to your chest.

Ramp-up sets for the first lift

  • Set 1: empty bar or very light weight, 10 reps.
  • Set 2: about 50% of your working weight, 6 reps.
  • Set 3: about 75% of your working weight, 3 reps. Then start your real sets.

  • Effort: 6 out of 10. You can talk in short sentences but not sing.
  • Heart rate: about 115 to 140 beats per minute.
  • Hands off the rails. Hold them only to balance, or the calorie burn drops a lot.
  • Build up: start at 8% incline in week 1 and add 1% each week until 10 to 12%.
  • Shoes and posture: look ahead, short natural steps, shoulders relaxed.

Short cardio after lifting (8 to 10 min)

5.5 km/h at 6% incline, steady. Finish by walking flat at 4 km/h for 2 minutes.

Optional intervals, from week 5

Once a week on Saturday: warm up 5 min, then 8 rounds of 30 seconds at 8 to 9 km/h (1% incline) and 90 seconds walking at 5 km/h. Cool down 5 min.

Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets. Add weight when you hit the top reps on every set. If something hurts (not just burns), stop and check your form or ask a coach.
